Foundry signature — iPhone setup

  1. Tap the Copy signature button below.
  2. Go to Settings → Mail → Signature.
  3. Paste. It will look plain — that's normal.
  4. Shake the phone, then tap Undo Change Attributes. The design comes back.
  5. Back out to save. Send yourself a test email to confirm.
